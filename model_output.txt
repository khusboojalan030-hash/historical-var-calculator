Why in-sample AUC is misleading: the model has already SEEN every
one of these points during training, so it can partly memorize noise
rather than learn the real pattern. That's why in-sample AUC is
almost always higher than test/CV AUC - the gap between them tells
you how much the model is overfitting. 5-fold CV is more reliable
than a single test split because it averages performance across 5
different train/test partitions, so it isn't just luck of one split.
